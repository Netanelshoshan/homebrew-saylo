Saylo 1.3.0

- Saylo no longer re-asks for Accessibility, microphone, or speech permissions on every launch or after an update — prompts now appear only when a permission is genuinely missing.

- The trigger key (fn) reliably stops a dictation session started from the menu bar, and a duplicate key-event path that could cut sessions short right after they began is gone.

- First-run model downloads now report themselves: pressing the trigger while a model is downloading or loading shows live progress instead of a silent "Preparing speech…".
